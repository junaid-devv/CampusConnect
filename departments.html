Academic Departments

Explore the diverse range of departments at COMSATS University, each committed to academic excellence and innovation.

Our Departments

COMSATS University offers programs across multiple disciplines

🖥️ Department of Computer Science

The Department of Computer Science offers BS, MS, and PhD programs in Computer Science, Software Engineering, and Artificial Intelligence. Students gain expertise in programming, data structures, algorithms, machine learning, and software development.

Programs: BS(CS), BS(SE), MS(CS), PhD(CS)

Head of Department: Dr. Ahmad Raza

⚡ Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering

This department focuses on electrical engineering, computer engineering, and telecommunications. Students learn about circuit design, signal processing, embedded systems, and power engineering through hands-on lab work and research.

Programs: BS(EE), BS(CE), MS(EE), PhD(EE)

Head of Department: Dr. Fatima Hussain

📊 Department of Business Administration

The Business Administration department offers comprehensive programs in management, finance, marketing, and entrepreneurship. Students develop leadership skills and business acumen through case studies and industry projects.

Programs: BBA, MBA, MS(Management), PhD(Management)

Head of Department: Dr. Usman Ali

🔬 Department of Mathematics

The Mathematics department covers pure mathematics, applied mathematics, and statistics. The department contributes to research in algebra, analysis, numerical methods, and mathematical modeling for real-world problems.

Programs: BS(Math), MS(Math), PhD(Math)

Head of Department: Dr. Sara Qureshi

🧪 Department of Physics

The Physics department provides programs that explore theoretical and experimental physics. Research areas include solid-state physics, nuclear physics, computational physics, and nanotechnology.

Programs: BS(Physics), MS(Physics), PhD(Physics)

Head of Department: Dr. Bilal Khan

🌍 Department of Humanities

The Humanities department offers programs in English, Social Sciences, and Education. The department focuses on developing critical thinking, communication skills, and cultural awareness among students.

Programs: BS(English), BS(Education), MS(English)

Head of Department: Dr. Nadia Parveen
