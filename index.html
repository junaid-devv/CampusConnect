About COMSATS University

Committed to excellence in education, research, and community service

COMSATS University Islamabad (CUI) is a leading public sector university in Pakistan, established in 1998. With campuses across the country, CUI offers undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral programs in engineering, computing, business, sciences, and humanities. The university is committed to providing quality education, fostering innovation, and contributing to national development through cutting-edge research.
