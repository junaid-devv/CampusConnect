Student Services

COMSATS University provides comprehensive support services to help students succeed in their academic journey.

Our Services

Dedicated to supporting every aspect of student life

📖 University Library

The COMSATS University Library houses over 100,000 books, journals, and digital resources. Students have access to online databases such as IEEE, ACM, Springer, and HEC Digital Library. The library provides reading rooms, computer labs, and group study areas.

Hours: Monday – Friday: 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM | Saturday: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM

Location: Central Library Building, Ground Floor

💰 Financial Aid Office

The Financial Aid Office administers scholarships, fee waivers, and need-based financial assistance programs. COMSATS offers HEC-funded scholarships, merit-based awards, and work-study opportunities to deserving students.

Available Aid: Merit Scholarships, Need-Based Aid, HEC Scholarships, Teaching Assistantships

Location: Admin Block, Room 203

🧠 Counseling Center

The Counseling Center offers confidential support for personal, academic, and career-related concerns. Licensed counselors are available for individual sessions, group workshops, and stress management programs.

Services: Personal Counseling, Academic Advising, Career Guidance, Stress Management Workshops

Location: Student Affairs Building, Room 105

💼 Career Services

The Career Services Office connects students with internship and job opportunities. Services include resume writing workshops, mock interviews, career fairs, and employer networking events throughout the academic year.

Services: Job Placement, Internship Matching, Resume Reviews, Career Fairs

Location: Student Affairs Building, Room 201

🏥 Health Center

The on-campus Health Center provides primary healthcare services, first aid, and health education. A qualified medical officer and nursing staff are available during campus hours for consultations and emergency care.

Services: General Checkups, First Aid, Health Awareness Campaigns, Referrals

Location: Health Center Building, Near Gate 2

🏋️ Sports & Recreation

COMSATS University offers a variety of sports facilities and recreational activities. Students can participate in cricket, football, badminton, table tennis, and annual inter-campus sports competitions.

Facilities: Sports Ground, Indoor Gym, Badminton Court, Table Tennis Room

Location: Sports Complex, West Campus

💻 IT Support

The IT Support Center assists students with network access, email setup, LMS (Learning Management System) issues, and computer lab access. Technical support is available on-campus and through an online helpdesk.

Services: WiFi Access, LMS Support, Email Setup, Lab Access

Location: IT Building, Room 101

🚌 Transport Service

COMSATS University provides a fleet of buses covering major routes across Islamabad and Rawalpindi. Students can purchase semester-based transport passes at subsidized rates from the Transport Office.

Routes: 15+ routes covering Islamabad & Rawalpindi

Location: Transport Office, Near Main Gate
